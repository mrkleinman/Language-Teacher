# Brief: review proposed Hong Kong local-context words

Input `input/R0N.csv`: candidate words (word, jyutping, meaning, pos, use_class, tier_hint, subgroup, english_slot, confidence, note, src) proposed for a Cantonese learner's list (Hong Kong usage, traditional characters, colloquial). Each will be shown to the learner with its Yale romanisation (converted from your Jyutping) and spoken by a voice from the written word, so a wrong reading or a word nobody says is a real harm.
Output `drafts/local/R0N-review.csv`, header exactly `word,verdict,jyutping_fixed,english_slot_fixed,note`, one row per input row, in input order:
- `verdict` = OK (word, reading, meaning and slot are right) / FIX (usable once the reading or the slot is corrected: fill jyutping_fixed and/or english_slot_fixed, standard Jyutping with tone numbers, syllables separated by spaces) / DROP (nobody in Hong Kong says it that way, the reading is unknowable, it is rude slang, written-only, or it is a duplicate of another row in the file - say which).
- Check EVERY reading syllable by syllable, including tone sandhi and the tone of the SPOKEN form (e.g. a changed tone 2 in 天橋 kiu2 is spoken, but list the standard dictionary tone unless the changed tone is the normal word reading). When two readings are both common, keep the commoner and say so in note.
- Be strict about what Hong Kong people really say today; the owner would rather have 450 right words than 600 doubtful ones. Be especially careful with: slang, loanwords written in Cantonese characters, place and brand names, compound nouns, sentence-final particles.
- `english_slot` must work in "this {E}" / "I want to {E}" / "{E} is so …" frames for its part of speech (nouns bare and countable where possible; verbs base form; adjectives bare); fix those that do not.
- Check with a script that every row is answered and every Jyutping in jyutping_fixed is syntactically valid (letters + tone digit per syllable). Do not edit any other file and do not commit. Report: counts OK / FIX / DROP, the 10 most important FIX or DROP decisions with one line each.
