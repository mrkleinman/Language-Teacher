# Cantonese (廣東話) word list and modules

Source of the word list: the shared Anki deck "Cantonese (粵語) HSK 1–5 — A Complete Colloquial Adaptation" (ankiweb.net/shared/info/1244813928, 2,501 words, Jyutping). Only the headword, Jyutping, English meaning and part of speech are used as a starting point; the deck's example sentences, audio and images are NOT copied into this repository (`input/` is git-ignored), and every module line is written from scratch.

Romanisation: Yale (converted from the deck's Jyutping by `tools/yale.py`, self-test included).
Characters: traditional (Hong Kong usage), colloquial register.
Belts: the same eleven belts and 2,500-word curriculum as the other languages; the order follows an everyday-use (80/20) classification, see `BRIEF_TAG.md`.

## Status (16 Nov 2026, after the gap fixes)
- Word list: **2,500 curriculum words** (deck words + 500 Hong Kong local-context words + 145 core words the deck lacked, minus 穿 and 睜, which are not Cantonese for what they teach, plus 32 words that had been cut for the local words), belts by position with no gap, ids locked (`id-lock.csv`). 當 is dōng "work as".
- Modules: **4,174 gap-fill modules** in `drafts/ALL-modules.csv` (merge with `python3 tools/yue_merge_modules.py`), checker clean (`python3 tools/yue_check.py drafts/ALL-modules.csv`). **Every word has at least three modules** (the TV01-TV08 top-up, 697 modules, written against `python3 tools/yue_topup_plan.py 8 TV all3`).
- In the app: language picker, Yale display, Hong Kong voice choice, embedded bank and modules, a module-driven Daily and Revision track, tests. Not yet: model-written Cantonese, Listening, Topics, Gen2, quality review (see APP-INTEGRATION.md "Slice 1 status").

## Beginner teaching order (17 Nov, stage A)
The first 200 positions (White 1-100, Orange 101-200: five new words a day for 40 days) are NOT the 80/20 usage order but a hand-made teaching order, `beginner-order.csv`, built so that every day's five words combine with the earlier days' words into real sentences a beginner can say (greetings and politeness first). Source `beginner-order-src.txt` (days, words, the proof sentences); `python3 tools/yue_beginner_order.py` checks the proof and writes `beginner-order.csv`, `framing-lines.csv` (opening / closing lines) and `BEGINNER-ORDER.md`; `tools/build_base.py` applies it (ids, tiers, the order of every other word untouched). `free-words.csv` (`tools/yue_free_words.py`) lists the words a sentence may use without them being taught (names, loanwords, interjections, sentence-final particles). `python3 tools/yue_stage_fit.py --write-md` counts, word by word, the existing modules a beginner could use and writes `starter-needs.csv` (stage B: the starter modules still to write). Rebuild order after a change: yue_beginner_order.py, build_base.py, yue_belts_doc.py, yue_beginner_order.py again (framing days), yue_stage_fit.py --write-md, then `python3 tools/build_yue_bank.py` and `python3 tools/build_yue_modules.py --fixture` from `dev/`.

Starter modules (stage B): a module whose id has the segment `-S<nn>` (`<CELL>-<tier>-S<nn>`) is a starter module and is checked against its own vocabulary (the words taught before the first word it fits + the free words + its slot; `python3 tools/yue_check.py starter-allowed WORD_ID` lists it). `python3 tools/yue_starter_plan.py` turns the stage-fit result into the eight writer batches `drafts/starter/SS01..SS08.csv` (read `BRIEF_STARTER.md`; writers produce `drafts/STARTER-SSnn-modules.csv`, merged by `tools/yue_merge_modules.py` like the TV files; `drafts/STARTER-SS00-sample-modules.csv` is a hand-written example that is deliberately not merged); `python3 tools/yue_starter_cov.py drafts/starter/SSnn.csv drafts/STARTER-SSnn-modules.csv` shows, per word, the usable modules (existing + starters). A tool that cuts a module's lines into words (the app build) calls `yue_check.module_cut(row, chunk)`, which applies the starter rule to a starter module and the tier list to every other.
