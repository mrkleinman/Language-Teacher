# The beginner teaching order of the first 200 words (White 1-100 and Orange 101-200: five new words a day, 40 days) and the sentences that prove it.
#
# Read by tools/yue_beginner_order.py, which checks every line and writes beginner-order.csv and BEGINNER-ORDER.md. Format:
#   day N                       starts a day
#   words: w1 w2 w3 w4 w5       the five words taught that day, in teaching order; `好@22` names the list row (id) when a spelling has two rows
#   <tokens> = <English>        one proof sentence: its Cantonese words separated by spaces (punctuation stays on the word before it). Every token must be a
#                               word of this day or an earlier day, or a free word (free-words.csv). Several short sentences may stand in one line.
# The tool rejects a sentence that uses a word taught later, a day with fewer than four distinct sentences, a day word that no sentence uses, and any Yale that does not validate.
# The principle: every day's five words combine with everything before them into things a person really says; greetings and politeness first, then the words that build
# sentences (pronouns, be, not, want, have, where, how much), then food, family, places, time; Hong Kong colloquial throughout (no Mandarin forms).

day 1
words: 你好 我 你 係 唔
你好！ = Hello!
你好，我係阿明。 = Hello, I am Ah Ming.
我係阿欣。 = I am Ah Yan.
你係阿明呀？ = Oh, you are Ah Ming?
我唔係阿明。 = I am not Ah Ming.
你係唔係阿欣？ = Are you Ah Yan?

day 2
words: 早晨 多謝 唔該 對唔住 再見
早晨！ = Good morning!
早晨，阿明！ = Good morning, Ah Ming!
多謝你。 = Thank you.
唔該！ = Thank you! / Excuse me! / Please!
對唔住，我唔係阿欣。 = Sorry, I am not Ah Yan.
多謝，再見！ = Thanks, goodbye!
再見，阿明！ = Goodbye, Ah Ming!

阿明係我嘅朋友。 = Ah Ming is my friend.
佢係唔係你嘅朋友？ = Is she your friend?
對唔住！ 唔緊要！ = Sorry! Never mind!
