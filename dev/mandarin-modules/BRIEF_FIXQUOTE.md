# Brief: rewrite the "quoting" phrase modules so the phrase is USED

Problem (owner's screenshots): modules for fixed expressions (すみません / 谢谢 / はい / いいえ / ようこそ ... the group X_PHRASE.*) were written so that the speakers TALK ABOUT the phrase ("I'll ask for directions. I'll say excuse me first." / "Good. It's best to say excuse me first and then ask."). A learner who repeats that aloud practises nothing. A fixed expression must be used as a real utterance in a small believable exchange: someone bumps into someone and says it, the other answers it, the first reacts. The English must be the real utterance ("Oh, sorry! Did that hurt?"), and the prompt says what to say ("Say sorry and ask if it hurt.").
Input: `drafts/fixquote/ids.txt` lists the module ids to rewrite (one per line). Find each id in the source files (`drafts/W*-modules.csv`, `drafts/T1-modules-remapped.csv`, `drafts/TOPUP-*-modules.csv`, `drafts/ZT*-modules.csv`: whichever holds it; never edit `ALL-modules.csv`). Rewrite the three lines (the Japanese / Mandarin, English and prompt of A1, B and A2) IN PLACE: keep the module_id, group, tier, belts, status and the CSV format (UTF-8 with BOM, same header), keep `exclude_ids` unless a word really does not fit, update job and scene to what the new exchange is.
Rules: the rules of the original brief (`BRIEF_WRITE.md`) still hold: closed vocabulary of the module's tier, length range, one coherent mini-conversation, A man / B woman, prompts say "her" for A and "him" for B, one speech act per prompt, no names, no "A"/"B", no line that asks and answers its own question, natural casual speech. The phrase `{W}` stays in every line, and it must read naturally for EVERY word of the cell that the module fits (use `render` on all of them): phrases of one cell (thanks, sorry, yes, no, greetings ...) share the exchange, so the exchange must suit all of them or exclude the odd one out. Never use the frames: 「{W}って言う」「{W}と言う」「说{W}」「别说{W}」「{W}这个词」「{W}という意味」. A phrase may appear in quotation only where a real person would quote it (rare; avoid).
Where a cell holds words that cannot be used as a plain utterance in one shared exchange (e.g. はい and いいえ answer a question: line 2 is the reply and opens with the word), design the exchange so line 1 asks a question that fits the whole cell and line 2 opens with `{W}`; exclude words that do not fit.
After editing run the cell's checker on the merged file (`python3 tools/<lang>_merge_modules.py` then `python3 tools/<lang>_check.py drafts/ALL-modules.csv --quiet`, the tool names are in tools/) until `problems 0` and `words with <3 modules` is still 0. Do not touch other modules. Do not commit. Report: modules rewritten, how many cells, any module you could not make natural for all words (and what you did), checker result.
