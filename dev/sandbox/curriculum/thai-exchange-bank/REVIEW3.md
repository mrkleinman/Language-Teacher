## 1. Cue grammar (every CUE line)
A cue is a complete, grammatical English instruction the learner hears before saying the Thai. Fix every cue that is not:
- "Say then you'll meet at the pier." → "Say that, in that case, you'll meet at the pier." · "Say then let's go to that shop." →
  "Suggest going to that shop, then." · "Say then see you tomorrow evening." → "Say goodbye: see you tomorrow evening, then."
- A fragment after Tell / Say: "Tell him two pieces." → "Tell him you'll take two pieces." · "Tell him but today is Tuesday." →
  "Point out that today is Tuesday." · "Tell her since this morning; you drank coffee …" → "Tell her it started this morning; you
  drank coffee …".
- The cue must say what the Thai says, no more, no less (no "even" when the Thai has no เลย; "don't" when the Thai is อย่า …;
  "let's" when the Thai is …กัน(นะ) as a suggestion). Keep the person rule: you / your = the speaker; the listener is her (A speaking)
  or him (B speaking). A good cue stays exactly as it is.

## 2. The target in genuinely different uses
Within one exchange, two lines must not use the target in the same collocation or the same frame (ปวดท้อง twice; กี่ชิ้น / สองชิ้น
both counting; คุ้มไหม / คุ้มมาก / คุ้มดี the same sense three times; ไป…กัน twice). Change ONE line so the word appears in a different,
natural use: another collocation (ท้องเสีย, ท้องว่าง), the negative (ไม่คุ้มเลย), a different object or function (ชิ้นละยี่สิบบาท;
แบ่งกันจ่าย). The exchange must stay coherent (B answers A; A follows up).

## 3. Specific exchanges flagged by the reviewers (fix these in the file that holds them)
- ขนาด (b09): clothes sizes are ไซซ์ in speech; put ขนาด where Thais say it (a room, a box, a drink: ห้องนี้ขนาดเท่าไหร่, ขอขนาดใหญ่).
- คู่ (b09): she says "buy two pairs" and he then buys another pair → three pairs; make the numbers agree.
- ชิ้น, คุ้ม (b09); กัน, หมื่น (b08: whose ticket / why she needs money must be clear inside the exchange); ท้อง (b23).
- หยิบ (b07): add เอง where the meaning is "myself" (ผมไปหยิบเองก็ได้ครับ).
- วันอังคาร (b03): the follow-up should read จริงด้วยครับ ผมลืมไปว่าวันนี้วันอังคาร (or equivalent), and the cue "Tell him but today is
  Tuesday." is a fragment.
- กำไร (b15): ได้กำไรกี่แสนคะ is the natural order; fix the cue to "Ask him how many hundred thousand baht they made in profit."
