NATURAL MANDARIN — mistakes seen before (write the natural form):
  - arrival: 你到了吗？ — not 你到不到？ (A-not-A asks about a habit or plan, not whether someone has arrived)
  - offering food: 你要吃一点吗？ / 吃一点吧 — not 你吃一点吗？
  - a sentence needs a reason to be said here: 你的钱很少吗？, 那个东西不快。, 家很小。 out of the blue are odd — say whose home / which thing, or make it a reply to the line before (这个车不快。 after a question about the car)
