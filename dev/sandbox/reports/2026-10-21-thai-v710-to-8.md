## What changed

### 1. The owner's v709 review

- **Medicine-bag / cup false positive.** The cue judges and the group check now know that in an instruction cue the learner plays the speaker. So "Tell him not yet; the medicine is in your bag" ↔ ยาอยู่ในกระเป๋า is correct, and so is "Tell him that cup is yours" ↔ ถ้วยนั้นของฉันค่ะ.
  - Tests: tests63 Z8 checks that both judges are told the rule, with both cases. Z9 checks that the bank's ยา and ถ้วย exchanges pass the group check.
  - On the owner's 30 words, no lines are replaced any more. The live v709 track had 7 replaced.
- **Only redundant bank-level judging is skipped.** A vetted line (unchanged bank text and bank cue) skips the paid judges that re-score it: the cue judge, the group check, the batch cue check and the scene critic. It still goes through every integrity check:
  - the early acceptance check (contract);
  - the closed-vocabulary gate;
  - the speaker gate;
  - length;
  - the fixed-verb rule (ดู = watch / look);
  - the final audit;
  - the coherence gate.

  If any stage changes a vetted line, it is judged again like any other line.
- **Whole-track coherence.** The setting no longer describes one café conversation; it says the lesson is a set of separate everyday exchanges. Coherence on the final tracks is 5/5.

### 3. Checker faults found by the paid tracks (fixed in the checks; vetted lines are not exempted)

Each of these dropped a vetted line in a real run and replaced it with a weaker model line. That broke the A–B–A exchange, which is why paid 14 scored 6.8.

- **Cue-form rule.**
  - A line that asks and then explains (มีกระดาษไหมครับ ผมจะเขียน…) is not a statement cued as a question.
  - "Ask her to … / for …" is a request.
- **Speaker check.** A marker inside the target word itself (ผม in ไดร์เป่าผม, a quoted สวัสดีครับ) does not belong to the speaker.
- **Fixed-verb rule.** ดู inside ฤดู, เลี้ยงดู, ฟังดู, น่าดู or มาให้ดู is not the verb "watch".
- **Track vocabulary gate.** ละ "per" (ใบละ, วันละ) is a grammar word.
- **Bank index.** 14 sentences appear in two exchanges, for example ได้กำไรกี่แสนคะ under both แสน and กำไร. The index kept only one entry per sentence, so under the other target the line was not recognised as vetted. Its section was then judged and rewritten. Paid 18 came out NOT_READY because of this.
- **Final translation fix and particle-spelling pass.** These left vetted lines' reviewed English and particles alone.
- **Opening and closing cues.** They use the Latin name (Nida, not นิดา) when a character is given Thai-first.

### 4. Romanisation

- **About 105 dictionary readings corrected** after an audit of every entry the bank uses: long แ aae, long ɔɔ aaw, long ʉʉ uue, missing tone marks such as bprà-, má-, rí, and ถวาย thà-wǎai.
- **Word breaks.**
  - ไม่ได้, ได้ไหม, เก็บไว้, ให้หน่อย and the reciprocal กัน are read as separate words.
  - A compound noun such as รถสองแถว stays one word.
  - The tens are one number word (สี่สิบ sìi-sìp).
  - The vetted closing ไว้เจอกันใหม่ reads wái jooe gan mài.
  - เช้า is cháo throughout.
